## 1. About LATCH

LATCH helps you locate and manage personal items using:

- The **LATCH mobile app** on an Android phone or iPhone.
- A **LATCH GPS tracker** attached to the item.
- An optional **NFC e-paper display** for contact, identification, or emergency information.

From the app, you can:

- View a tracker's current or last known location.
- Check its connection, GPS, battery, and cellular-signal status.
- Review location history and recent activity.
- Create geofences and receive alerts.
- Update an NFC e-paper display without using a cable or battery in the display.
- Manage your profile, password, notification settings, and logged-in devices.

### Important terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Item** | The object you are tracking, such as a bag, bicycle, or equipment case. |
| **Tracker** | The physical LATCH GPS device attached to the item. |
| **Claim code** | The code used to add a tracker to your account. It looks like `LATCH-XXXX-XXXX`. |
| **Last known location** | The most recent valid location received from the tracker. It may not be the tracker's current position. |
| **Geofence** | A virtual boundary around a location that can trigger entry or exit alerts. |
| **NFC e-paper display** | A reusable monochrome display updated by holding a supported phone against it. |

> **Important:** A claim code is different from the tracker's internal device ID. Enter only the `LATCH-XXXX-XXXX` code supplied with your tracker when adding it in the app.

## 2. Before You Begin

### Phone requirements

- **Android:** Android 7.0 or later with NFC for e-paper writing.
- **iPhone:** iOS 15 or later with NFC for e-paper writing.
- Internet access through Wi-Fi or mobile data.
- Location and notification permissions when you want to use the related features.

### Tracker requirements

- A registered LATCH tracker with its claim code.
- A charged battery.
- Cellular service in the area.
- A reasonably open view of the sky when acquiring a GPS location.

### First-use checklist

1. Charge the tracker.
2. Install and open the LATCH app.
3. Create an account or sign in with Google.
4. Verify your email address if the app asks you to do so.
5. Allow the permissions needed for the features you plan to use.
6. Add the tracker using its claim code.
7. Place the tracker where it can receive both GPS and cellular signals.

## 3. Create an Account and Sign In

### Create an account with email

1. Open the LATCH app.
2. Select **Create account** or **Sign up**.
3. Enter your name, email address, and password.
4. Submit the form.
5. Open the verification message sent to your email address.
6. Follow the verification instructions, then return to the app and sign in.

Passwords must be 8 to 72 characters and contain at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one symbol.

### Continue with Google

1. Select **Continue with Google** on the sign-in screen.
2. Choose the Google account you want to use.
3. Review and approve the requested sign-in information.
4. Wait for the app to open your LATCH account.

Use the same sign-in method and email address each time to avoid creating a different account by mistake.

### Sign in with email and password

1. Enter your registered email address.
2. Enter your password.
3. Select **Sign in**.

Your session normally remains active on the phone. For security, a session can expire and require you to sign in again.

### Reset a forgotten password

1. On the sign-in screen, select **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. Open the password-reset message sent to your inbox.
4. Follow the link and set a new password.
5. Return to the app and sign in with the new password.

Check the spam or junk folder if the message does not arrive. Also confirm that the email address was entered correctly.

## 4. Navigate the App

The app has four main tabs:

| Tab | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Items** | View, add, locate, and manage your trackers. |
| **E-Paper** | Create a layout and write it to an NFC e-paper display. |
| **Notifications** | Read alerts and review recent notification activity. |
| **Profile** | Manage your account, preferences, security, and logged-in devices. |

Common controls include:

- **Back:** Return to the previous screen without leaving your account.
- **Refresh:** Request the latest available information.
- **Save:** Apply the changes you made.
- **Cancel:** Close the current action without saving.
- **More options:** Open actions such as rename or remove.

### App permissions

LATCH may request the following permissions:

- **Location:** Used for map-related features and location-based assistance.
- **Notifications:** Used to show geofence, battery, connection, and other alerts.
- **NFC:** Required when writing an e-paper display.
- **Photos or camera:** Used only when you select or capture an image for your profile or an e-paper layout.

If a feature does not work after you previously denied a permission, open the phone's system settings, find LATCH, and enable the required permission.

## 5. Add a Tracker

You must claim a tracker before it appears in your Items list.

### Add an item

1. Open the **Items** tab.
2. Select **Add item** or the add button.
3. Enter the claim code supplied with the tracker. The format is `LATCH-XXXX-XXXX`.
4. Enter a recognizable name, such as `Work Backpack` or `Blue Bicycle`.
5. Confirm the details and submit.
6. Wait for the item to appear in your list.

Claim codes may be case-sensitive. Enter the code exactly as printed and keep all hyphens.

### If the claim code is rejected

- Check for typing mistakes, especially `0` and `O`, or `1` and `I`.
- Confirm that you entered the claim code, not a serial number or internal tracker ID.
- Make sure the tracker has not already been claimed by another account.
- Check your internet connection and try again.
- Contact LATCH support if the correct unused code continues to fail.

## 6. View and Manage an Item

### Open item details

1. Open the **Items** tab.
2. Select an item card.
3. Review the map, status information, and available actions.

### Understand location labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Current** | The tracker recently reported a valid location. |
| **Last known** | The app is showing the most recent valid location, but it may be old. Check its timestamp. |
| **Unavailable** | No valid location is available yet. |

Always read the location timestamp. A pin can remain visible even when the tracker is no longer at that position.

### Understand connection labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Online** | The tracker communicated recently. |
| **Stale** | The tracker has not communicated within the expected interval. |
| **Offline** | The tracker has been disconnected for a longer period. |
| **Unknown** | The app does not yet have enough information to determine its state. |

GPS location and cellular connection are related but different. A tracker may be online while still waiting for a valid GPS fix, especially indoors.

### Battery, GPS, and signal indicators

- **Battery** shows the latest reported tracker battery level.
- **GPS/GNSS** shows whether a valid satellite location is available.
- **Signal** shows the latest cellular signal information when provided by the tracker.

These values come from the latest tracker report. Use the accompanying timestamp to decide whether they are still current.

### Refresh item information

1. Open the item details.
2. Select **Refresh**.
3. Wait for the app to retrieve the latest data available from the service.

Refreshing the app cannot force an offline tracker to connect immediately. If the tracker has no battery, cellular service, or GPS view, the app may continue to show the previous report.

### Rename an item

1. Open the item details or its **More options** menu.
2. Select **Rename** or **Edit name**.
3. Enter the new name.
4. Select **Save**.

Choose a name that you can recognize quickly in alerts, such as `Jansdale's Backpack` instead of `Tracker 1`.

### Remove a tracker from your account

1. Open the item details or its **More options** menu.
2. Select **Remove device**.
3. Read the confirmation message.
4. Confirm only if you want to release the tracker from your account.

Removing a tracker stops it from appearing in your account. It is different from deleting your entire LATCH account.

## 7. Use Location History

Location History shows validated tracker positions over time.

1. Open an item.
2. Select **Location History**.
3. Choose the available date or time range.
4. Select a point to view its timestamp and details.

The default view covers the most recent 24 hours. Available location history is retained for up to 30 days.

### Read history correctly

- A line between points represents the reporting sequence; it is not always the exact route traveled.
- Gaps can occur when the tracker is indoors, has weak cellular service, is turned off, or has a low battery.
- Ignore impossible jumps and check the timestamps before drawing conclusions.
- A location is accepted only after the system validates the report, but normal GPS variation can still occur.

## 8. Create and Manage Geofences

A geofence is a circular virtual boundary around a selected location.

### Create a geofence

1. Open the item you want to monitor.
2. Open **Geofences**.
3. Select **Add geofence**.
4. Choose the center point on the map or use a searched location.
5. Enter a clear name, such as `Home` or `School`.
6. Set the radius.
7. Enable the entry and exit alerts you want.
8. Select **Save**.

The supported radius is 50 to 5,000 meters. A radius of about 100 meters is a practical starting point for many outdoor locations.

### Edit, deactivate, or delete a geofence

1. Open the item's **Geofences** list.
2. Select the geofence.
3. Choose **Edit**, change its active state, or select **Delete**.
4. Save or confirm the action.

Deactivating a geofence keeps its settings but stops new entry and exit alerts. Deleting it removes the saved boundary.

### Improve geofence reliability

- Place the center at the actual property or area you want to monitor.
- Avoid an extremely small radius in locations with buildings, trees, or weak GPS reception.
- Allow for normal GPS variation near the boundary.
- Confirm that tracker and app notifications are enabled.
- Check the event timestamp before reacting to an alert.

## 9. Notifications and Activity

LATCH can notify you about events such as:

- Geofence entry or exit.
- Low tracker battery.
- Tracker connection or disconnection changes.
- Other device or account events supported by the current app version.

### Read notifications

1. Open the **Notifications** tab.
2. Select an alert to view its details.
3. Note the item name, event type, and timestamp.
4. Open the related item when you need current status or location information.

An alert describes the event recorded at its timestamp. Open the item and refresh it before assuming that the same condition is still active.

### Change notification preferences

1. Open **Profile**.
2. Open **Settings** or **Notification settings**.
3. Turn each available notification category on or off.
4. Save the changes if prompted.

The phone's operating system can block notifications even when they are enabled inside LATCH. Check both the app settings and the phone's notification settings.

### Review activity history

The activity timeline records important account and tracker events. Activity records are available for up to 365 days.

Use the timeline to check what happened and when. Location History is the better view when you need a map of tracker positions.

## 10. Use the NFC E-Paper Display

The LATCH e-paper feature creates a 240 × 416 monochrome layout and writes it to a compatible display using NFC.

The display keeps its last image without continuous power. NFC is needed only when changing that image.

### Prepare the phone and display

1. Make sure the phone supports NFC.
2. Enable NFC on Android. On supported iPhones, the app starts the NFC session when needed.
3. Remove a thick or metal phone case if it interferes with NFC.
4. Place the e-paper display on a flat, non-metal surface.
5. Identify the phone's NFC area. It is commonly near the top of an iPhone and varies by Android model.

### Start a layout

1. Open the **E-Paper** tab.
2. Choose a starting option:
   - **Contact Information**
   - **Emergency Contact**
   - **Identification**
   - **Custom Message**
   - **Blank Canvas**
3. Enter or edit the information shown in the layout.

Do not place passwords, financial information, private medical details, or other sensitive content on a publicly visible display.

### Edit the layout

Depending on the selected layout, you can:

- Add or edit text.
- Add an image.
- Move and resize supported elements.
- Review the monochrome preview.
- Return to a template or blank canvas when you want to start again.

High-contrast images and short text are easiest to read on a monochrome display. Review the complete preview before writing.

### Write the display

1. Finish the layout and select **Write to display**.
2. Follow the on-screen NFC instructions.
3. Hold the phone's NFC area directly against the display's NFC area.
4. Keep both devices still and maintain contact during the entire transfer.
5. Wait for the app to report success before moving the phone.
6. Allow the display to finish refreshing, then confirm that the new image is complete.

The screen may flash during an e-paper refresh. This is normal.

### If the NFC write is interrupted

1. Keep the display still.
2. Move the phone away for a moment.
3. Start the write again in the app.
4. Reposition the phone carefully over the NFC area.
5. Hold it steady until the app confirms completion.

A message such as **Tag lost** means the NFC connection ended before the transfer finished. It does not automatically mean that the display is damaged.

### NFC placement tips

- Move the phone slowly until the app detects the display.
- Once detected, stop moving it.
- Avoid metal tables and magnetic accessories.
- Remove cases containing metal plates, cards, or ring holders.
- Keep the app open and the phone unlocked.
- If repeated attempts fail, restart the phone and try again.

## 11. Profile and Account Security

### Update your profile

1. Open **Profile**.
2. Select **Edit profile**.
3. Change the available details.
4. Select **Save**.

Profile photos can use JPEG, PNG, or WebP format and must not exceed 5 MiB.

### Change your password

1. Open **Profile** and then the password or security section.
2. Enter your current password if requested.
3. Enter and confirm the new password.
4. Save the change.

Use a unique password that you do not reuse on another service. The password must meet the rules described in [Create an Account and Sign In](#3-create-an-account-and-sign-in).

### Review logged-in devices

1. Open **Profile**.
2. Open **Logged-in devices**, **Sessions**, or the equivalent security option.
3. Review the recognizable device name and recent session information.
4. Remove a session you do not recognize.

Refreshing the current session updates its device information without requiring you to log out. Ending another session signs that device out.

If you see an unfamiliar session, remove it and change your password immediately.

### Log out

- Use **Log out** to end the current phone's session.
- Use the session-management screen when you want to sign out another device.

Logging out does not delete your items or account.

### Delete your account

1. Open the account or security settings.
2. Select **Delete account**.
3. Read the warning carefully.
4. Complete the confirmation steps.

Account deletion permanently removes the LATCH account and its owned claim data according to the service deletion process. It cannot be undone in the app. If you cannot sign in, use the public Account Deletion page or contact support.

## 12. Tracker Care and Status Lights

### Status lights

| Light | Pattern | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Blue** | Solid | A recent valid GPS location is available. |
| **Blue** | Blinking | The tracker is trying to acquire a GPS location. |
| **Yellow** | Solid | The tracker is registered on the cellular network. |
| **Yellow** | Blinking | The tracker is searching for a cellular network. |
| **Red** | Solid | The battery is charging. |
| **Red** | Blinking | The battery is low. |
| **Green** | Solid | Charging is complete. |

Light behavior may overlap while the tracker is charging, connecting, and finding its location.

### Charging and normal use

- Use a suitable USB power source and an undamaged cable.
- Charge the tracker in a dry, ventilated location.
- Do not use a swollen, leaking, unusually hot, or physically damaged battery.
- After disconnecting USB power, allow time for the tracker to reconnect and obtain a location.
- A tracker connected only to USB may enter charge-only behavior. Check normal tracking again after returning it to battery operation.

### Placement tips

- Keep the tracker away from large metal surfaces when possible.
- Do not completely enclose it in signal-blocking material.
- For the first location fix, place it outdoors with a clear view of the sky.
- Keep it dry unless the tracker enclosure is specifically rated for water exposure.
- Secure it so it cannot strike hard surfaces or disconnect from its battery.

### Routine checks

- Check the battery before a trip or important use.
- Confirm that the app shows a recent report.
- Test geofence notifications before relying on them.
- Inspect the enclosure, cable, and attachment point.
- Update the e-paper contact information when it changes.

## 13. Privacy and Data

LATCH processes account, tracker, location, notification, and session information needed to provide its features.

### Default history periods

| Information | Availability in the app |
|---|---|
| Validated location history | Up to 30 days |
| Activity history | Up to 365 days |

### Privacy practices for users

- Do not share your password or verification messages.
- Store the tracker claim code securely until the tracker is added.
- Remove sessions you do not recognize.
- Avoid displaying private information on the e-paper screen.
- Remove a tracker from your account before transferring ownership.
- Delete exported screenshots or location information that you no longer need.

## 14. Troubleshooting

### App and account

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Cannot sign in | Check the email and password, confirm internet access, and use **Forgot password?** if needed. |
| Verification email did not arrive | Check spam, confirm the email address, wait briefly, and request another message if available. |
| Google sign-in opens the wrong account | Sign out of the unwanted Google account or explicitly select the registered account. |
| App data does not refresh | Check internet access, reopen the item, and remember that an offline tracker cannot provide a new report. |
| Notifications do not appear | Enable the category in LATCH and allow notifications in the phone's system settings. |

### Tracker and location

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Tracker is offline | Charge it, move to an area with cellular service, and wait for the yellow network light to stabilize. |
| Location is unavailable | Move outdoors, give the tracker a clear sky view, and wait for the blue GPS light to stabilize. |
| Location is old | Check the timestamp, tracker battery, and connection status; then refresh later. |
| Battery percentage seems old | Check the report timestamp. A new value appears only after the tracker communicates. |
| Geofence alert is late or missing | Check GPS and cellular coverage, increase a very small radius, and verify notification settings. |
| Claim code is rejected | Re-enter the `LATCH-XXXX-XXXX` code exactly, confirm it is unused, and contact support if it still fails. |

### NFC e-paper

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Display is not detected | Enable NFC, remove interfering accessories, and slowly reposition the phone over the display's NFC area. |
| **Tag lost** appears | Restart the write and keep the phone completely still until completion. |
| Write stops repeatedly | Try a non-metal surface, remove the case, restart the phone, and try again. |
| Display image is incomplete | Repeat the full write without moving the phone, then wait for the display refresh to finish. |
| Preview is difficult to read | Use shorter text, larger elements, and higher-contrast images. |

### Before contacting support

Record the following when possible:

- Your phone model and operating-system version.
- LATCH app version.
- The item's display name.
- The time the problem occurred, including time zone.
- The exact error message.
- A screenshot that does not expose your password or claim code.
- Tracker light patterns and whether it was on battery or USB power.
- The steps you already tried.
